## Background

Certain parts can't be removed without draining the fluids first.
However, once drained, the engine can't be started — so any tasks requiring a running engine must be completed beforehand.
That task is the fuel pressure release.
I realized this too late and ended up having to refill 3.6L of fresh oil after already draining it. A major blunder.

## Reflections

Relatively simple prep work, nothing special. The coupler removal tool "Kaputoru" has been a lifesaver lately — made disconnecting the opening relay coupler easy.

## Fuel Pressure Release

This must be done before disconnecting fuel hoses, otherwise gasoline sprays out — dangerous.
Without removing the column covers, I did the coupler disconnect by feel while watching on my phone camera.

1. Disconnect the opening relay coupler under the steering column
2. Crank and confirm the engine stalls
3. Crank 2–3 more times

## Coolant Drain

I hadn't touched the cooling system since purchase, but the drained coolant was clean, and the interior visible through the cap looked pristine.
I'd heard the NA Roadster's radiator drain is a Phillips screw from factory, but mine had some kind of cock-type drain instead.
No way it would loosen by hand, so I used pliers with a rubber sheet.
I'd like to replace it with the OEM part.
