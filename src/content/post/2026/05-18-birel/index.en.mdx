Since it's been sitting neglected, I want to disassemble as much as possible — specifically, split the crankcase halves.
One side has the drive sprocket, the other has the magneto rotor.
Think of it like a pulley on a motorcycle — it needs to come off.
I'd rather use general-purpose tools than kart-specific specialty tools.
So I bought an external bearing puller.
But the thickness prevented proper grip, and the plan failed...
