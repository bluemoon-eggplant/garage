The rear shaft not turning was, as expected, due to seized calipers.
Removing the caliper revealed pads with plenty of material left — but that's not the issue here.

<Image {...IMAGE_SIZES.FIXED.MDX_LG} src={Img1} alt="" />

I proceeded with disassembly, found what seemed to be a drain bolt by feel, and tried pushing the pistons out with water pressure.
One side came out.
I reinserted the freed piston and tried applying water pressure to the other side, but the seal in the freed piston was done — no pressure could build. Gave up.

For the severely seized caliper, I tried:

* Heating with a torch and turning with a piston tool
* Removing everything possible and hammering the piston from behind with a drill bit
* Using a spring compressor to push the piston in and break the seizure

None of it worked.
Had to discard one of the calipers...
