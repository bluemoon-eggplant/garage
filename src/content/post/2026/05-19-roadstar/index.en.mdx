## Background

Before jacking up the car, I want to loosen any stubborn bolts for safety.
Starting with the crankshaft bolt.
This bolt relies on the vehicle's weight on the ground to prevent the crank from spinning, so it must be done before jacking up.

## Reflections

Glad I managed to tackle the toughest bolt.
Also glad that none of the plastic parts broke during removal.
On the other hand, some parts were harder than expected to remove — a lesson in doing more research beforehand.

## Air Intake Duct Removal

Yep, the accordion section of the pipe was split. (Apparently a common issue.)

## Crankshaft Bolt

Handbrake on, 5th gear engaged as a rotation stop.
After that, it's pure brute force.
Bigger is better — after a few minutes of wrestling with an extendable ratchet handle, I got it loose.

<Image {...IMAGE_SIZES.FIXED.MDX_LG} src={Img3} alt="" />

## Air Cleaner Box Removal

The box itself comes off easily, but the airflow sensor coupler on it wouldn't budge at all.
After prying with no success, I looked it up and found you need to remove a thin retaining wire first.
This is even explained in the "Roadster Maintenance Book" — my fault for not checking beforehand.
