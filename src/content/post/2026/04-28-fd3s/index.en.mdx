## Background

I'd actually booked this since late last year, and the shop finally had an opening.  
The hood had its edge scraped from the wiper blade hitting it, and the rear bumper had bumps and cracked paint that had been bothering me for a while.  
With material and labor costs rising, I decided to get it done while I still could.  
According to the mechanic, automotive paint materials are genuinely running scarce — seems like I just made it in time.

## Work Done

Full repaint of both hood and rear bumper after removal.  
The hood got new washer nozzles, and the rear got new emblems. (The nozzles were discontinued, so I sourced them online and brought them in myself.)  

## Impressions

They did a beautiful job.  
The condition wasn't terrible to begin with, but fresh emblems really do make a difference. (Got caught in the rain on the way home, though.)
